## `eslint-plugin-no-unsanitized/property` against its own contract

Their documentation: *"disallows unsafe assignment to innerHTML and similar DOM properties"*

<https://github.com/mozilla/eslint-plugin-no-unsanitized>

Every form below is that shape.

| form | theirs | ours |
|---|---|---|
| innerHTML assignment — `el.innerHTML = x;` | 1 | 1 |
| outerHTML assignment — `el.outerHTML = x;` | 1 | 1 |
| computed innerHTML — `el["innerHTML"] = x;` | **missed** | 1 |
| template-literal computed key — `el[`innerHTML`] = x;` | **missed** | 1 |
| insertAdjacentHTML — `el.insertAdjacentHTML('beforeend', x);` | **missed** | 1 |

**3 of 5** documented forms are not reported by their rule.
